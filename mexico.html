Getting there

A few more details to help you plan the trip.

Flights: Manzanillo International Airport (ZLO) is the closest airport. We recommend booking as soon as you can — February is high season in this part of Mexico.

Shuttle: We'll likely be arranging a group shuttle from Puerto Vallarta International Airport (PVR) to Manzanillo based on dates and times that we are informed of through the RSVP form. There is also a comfortable bus available but the trip is long from Puerto Vallerta to Manzanillo, so be warned.

Passports: Make sure yours is valid for at least six months past the wedding date.

Transportation once in Manzanillo

There is a private car service that we always have agreat experience with in Manzanillo. Prices are good for using this method of transportation exclusively during your stay. We are likely to rent a few cars as well so between those two methods all of your needs should be covered. As the trip draws nearer an email chain and text group will be created for getting everyone from the airport to Club Santiago.

About the area

Manzanillo

Located halfway down the Pacific side of Mexico. A port city, the largest in the Mexican state of Colima. Host to Mexico's largest port.

Club Santiago

You'll want to stay within Club Santiago during your trip! It is a gated community on the northwest side of the city. Many Americans and Canadians frequent the vacacion homes within the club. The Zirnhelt family has been coming here for a long time - since Tony Zirnhelt (Maggie's dad) was a kid!
